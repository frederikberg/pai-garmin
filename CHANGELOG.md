## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The Alarm sub-app: create, edit and delete one-off alarms from the watch, and stop a ringing alarm by opening the app. The time entry for a new alarm starts at the current time and shows it while you pick.
- The Weather sub-app: the chance of rain in the next hour and the next twelve, coloured by verdict, with place and location age. Start refreshes.
- Weather pages: Down shows the next twelve hours as bars (height the chance, colour how hard it rains) with the heaviest hour and the total amount, Down again the radar picture half an hour ahead; Up pages back. The rows name the heaviest hour and the amount, and every line shrinks its font until it fits the round screen.
- The Computer screen, for watches with Toybox.Audio: opening it from the menu starts a call with the server. The microphone starts when the screen opens, before the call is up, and the open goes out once it has delivered; the screen says *Wait* until then and *Speak*, with one short buzz, when the microphone delivers; the watch records each turn by itself as Ogg Opus 16 kHz and streams it while you speak, Start sends the turn or cuts the answer and records again, the answer plays piece by piece as it arrives (Ogg Opus, one sentence a piece, each through its own audio source) with what the server heard on screen while it thinks and the sentence being spoken while it plays, the watch's own buzzes say that the microphone delivers, that the turn was sent, that any other press was taken (a short tap), that the call is over and that it stopped for an error, Up and Down change the volume, which is remembered, Back hangs up and returns to the menu once the server has heard it (two seconds at most), and an Up hold offers a new call. Requests are repeated with growing waits, the call resumes after a gap or is replaced when the server forgot it, and every error and retry goes to the server's log for the call.
- A "Mic filter" app setting (off by default) for noise and echo reduction on the microphone; the filters used are logged. The next turn starts as soon as the last piece has played, and a stall of the app's own clock longer than 1.5 s is logged. While you speak the watch asks the server for news only every five seconds and sends the audio about a second at a time, so nothing stands between the press and the answer; the microphone reports in small windows, so the buzz for *speak* comes sooner.
- A "Debug log" app setting (on in this beta): a line for every request and the capture states go to the server's log; piece fetches rank above played reports. Every turn leaves a summary line with its timings (microphone start, press to sent, to the first piece, to the first sound), and the log only travels while an answer plays or nothing is going on. The log also goes to `POST /api/watch/log` while no call is up and, for the lines a previous run kept, at launch.
- The Computer screen is one word in a ring of the state's colour (grey wait, green speak, yellow sending and thinking, blue speaking, red stopped) and is drawn again only when that picture changes. The lines a run could not send lead the next run's log, with an exit line saying whether Back or the system ended the app and after how long.
- Computer keeps a turn short: the press rides on the last audio request, played reports ride on the next request, and the answer's first piece is asked for before it is announced. The server ends a call after three minutes without a word in a turn the watch records; the watch then lets what plays finish, buzzes bye and returns to the menu. A call that Computer hands to a session shows only its state and the session's name, inside the round display; Back there returns to Computer, sending what was being recorded into the session's draft, unsent.
- A home menu with Computer (where the watch has audio), Weather and Alarm that appears at once; a check for a ringing alarm runs behind it, and a sub-app's request waits for that check.
- A glance that shows the next alarm, or that one rings.
- Server URL and watch key as app settings in Garmin Connect.
